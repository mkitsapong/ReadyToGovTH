# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Both job seekers and employers. Job seekers use the platform to find and apply for jobs, while employers use it to post job openings and manage candidates.

## Product Purpose
To serve as a general job board that aggregates job listings, making them easy for job seekers to search and filter to find relevant opportunities.

## Positioning
An accessible and efficient platform that connects job seekers with employers through aggregated listings and robust search/filter capabilities.

## Operating Context
Users access the platform through a web browser to search for jobs, filter results, and explore opportunities. Employers use the platform to post and manage their listings.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Stack:** React, Vite, React Router, Firebase.
- **Constraints:** The existing Firebase backend architecture, schema, and current functional features must be preserved. Visual redesigns are allowed as long as they do not break backend integration.

## Principles
- **Searchability:** Finding relevant jobs should be fast, intuitive, and highly filterable.
- **Clarity:** Job descriptions and employer information must be presented cleanly and consistently.
- **Stability:** The frontend may evolve visually, but the backend data structures remain a fixed foundation.
